What good branding really costs

Strategy · 5 min read · 12 March 2026 · By Author Name

Branding costs vary widely because the work varies widely. A name and logo is a different job from a full identity with guidelines, packaging and rollout.

The biggest costs are thinking and time: research, testing ideas and refining them with your team. The files you receive are the smallest part.

A closer look

Ask what is included, how many rounds of feedback you get, and who owns the final work. Those answers explain most price differences.

The cheapest brand is the one you only have to build once.

Three things to try

  • Set a range before you ask for quotes.
  • Compare scope, not just price.
  • Budget for rollout, not only design.
